02 / Controller

Keep the deadline
outside the evidence test.

BG-D preserves the reports, scores, cache and evidence thresholds. It changes where the fallback is checked: every physical step, whether reports are absent, weak, or inconclusive. Evidence may bring a switch forward; it cannot postpone the deadline.

The report

Agents near the gate send their axis, distance, and a quantized waiting-progress residual. Old reports receive exponentially decreasing weight.

The decision

On report boundaries, the original evidence rule can authorize an early switch. A separate physical-time clock enforces the maximum phase duration.

The bound

For any delivery pattern, an axis has at most H + c uninterrupted steps without green, where c is clearance and H > c. This is a service interruption bound, not a bound on an agent's queue wait.

04 / Scope

Service frequency
has a switching cost.

A short deadline works well when switching is cheap and demand alternates. Long phases can be useful under sustained imbalance. Keeping a fallback active and choosing its duration are distinct design decisions.

Why use greedy movement in the main comparison?

Routes are fixed and legal movement has no energy or risk penalty. Greedy movement removes avoidable action hesitation. Learned PPO movement and the optional report-conditioned logit gain appear as secondary controls, not as the cause of the deadline result.

What exactly is p95 waiting?

Waiting subtracts the 48-step free-flow traversal from each visit's ready-to-completion time. The reported metric is the mean of realization-level 95th percentiles, not a pooled percentile. The scoring cohort includes every visit becoming ready in the scoring window.

What does the service bound leave open?

It bounds an axis's uninterrupted loss of green under the stated two-axis model. It does not bound individual queue waits or establish network stability. The experiments use one junction, with no downstream spillback, transport delay, or failure of the reliable phase-status interface.

How much does the residual byte add?

With separately tuned scheduling controls, distance-weighted presence is close to the waiting-progress residual: stress-case p95 changes by +0.5 steps, [−0.2, 1.2]. The residual saturates frequently at high load. The deadline comparison does not require changing its precision.
